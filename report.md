## 2. Processing Plant Economics — Concentrate Production

### 2.1 Process Flowsheet (Flake-Preserving Design)

```
ROM Ore (4.86% TGC) → Primary Crush (Jaw) → Secondary Crush (HPGR) → Rod Mill → Ball Mill
→ Rougher Flotation (8 cells) → Cleaner Flotation (3 stages, Column cells)
→ Recleaner (2 stages) → Thickener → Pressure Filter → Rotary Dryer
→ Ultrasonic Sizing (+50, +80, +100, +200, -200 mesh) → FIBC Bagging
```

**Key Flake Preservation Decisions (Revenue Impact +20-30%):**
- **HPGR over Cone Crusher:** +$5-8M capex, +5-10% coarse flake recovery
- **Rod Mill over Ball Mill (primary):** +$3-5M capex, less flake breakage
- **Column Cleaner Cells:** +20% flotation capex, +3-5% coarse flake recovery
- **Open Circuit Grinding:** Minimises recirculation damage
