## Implementation order

1. **Capture the current shape and registration.** Save full-site, both-pit, North and South views. Orbit each pit from both sides and inspect the crest at close range. Record the two footprint bounds and centers, source cell supports, floor elevations, grade band counts and visible block sets. Inspect the openings with imagery temporarily off and with mesh edges visible in a diagnostic view so that missing triangles are distinguishable from dark textures.
2. **Cut each opening on its actual outline.** Keep the continuous North and South envelopes in their current RF1 locations. Split only the terrain triangles intersecting each footprint, retaining the portion outside the footprint and interpolating UV, color and elevation attributes from the original terrain triangles. Preserve untouched triangles outside the affected area. Collect the resulting boundary vertices in order, using the displayed terrain triangles to determine their exact elevations.
3. **Build the rim and benches from shared vertices.** Reuse the opening boundary positions directly as the pit crest. Connect the crest to the first bench with a graded wall that stays below the original ground. Remove the global lift at the join. Reuse each interior bench ring for the bands on both sides of it. Give treads and walls separate shading where needed while keeping their shared edges coincident. If a transition locally has too little room to fit a tread, resolve it within that wall segment and flag it in the geometry audit. Keep the bench shape clearly identified as conceptual.
4. **Swap terrain and pit geometry together.** Keep paired original and excavated terrain indices for the comparison control. Publish any higher-detail terrain and its rebuilt pit rims together, with matching depth occluders. Keep the original cut state until both replacement rims are ready. During the guided sequence, show intact terrain before excavation and apply the cut in the same update that makes its pit surface visible. Avoid rebuilding either pit on orbit, zoom or grade filter changes.
5. **Verify the grade cells against the display.** Keep the current source masks, grade filters and cell coordinates. Compare the cell faces against the stitched surface and report intersections and apparent daylighting by pit. Inspect the current count of 12,026 loaded grade cells by source mask and the separate visible counts for each preset. If a source cell intersects the conceptual bench, keep its source position and use a clearly identified inspection section to show its extent. Do not move the source data to follow a cosmetic bench.
