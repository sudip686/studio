> How the video moves from intro to close. Reuse as the section order for any project
> presentation. Each beat = claim → number → on-screen proof → door left open.

## Act 1 — Place (00:00-00:25) · frames 0003/0010/0020
Brand card → globe → Kazakhstan + flag → site pinpoint. Message: established mining
jurisdiction, between Europe and China, ~190km from Karaganda. Job: orient + legitimise.

## Act 2 — Why it matters (00:25-00:45) · frame 0030
Batteries macro → graphite irreplaceable in anodes → demand doubling this decade.
Job: create urgency before any rock appears.

## Act 3 — Geology → resource (00:40-01:30) · frames 0060/0070/0080
Strata (Carboniferous shales/sandstones) → synclines → pluton heat → metamorphism →
two zones (Central/North) → **229Mt @ 28.9% TGC**, "largest and highest-grade globally".
Job: mechanism first, then the number lands as inevitable.

## Act 4 — Mine plan (01:26-01:55) · frames 0095/0105
150ktpa ore, **0.2:1 strip**, **8.6Mt @ 30% reserve**, 60-yr life consuming **4%** of resource.
Job: small footprint, huge optionality ("generations of expansion").

## Act 5 — Beneficiation (01:54-02:25) · frames 0125/0130
Haul → crush/mill to <15 micron → flotation → **50ktpa @ 80-85% C**, dried, 3 fractions.
Job: simple, proven flowsheet; product exists before downstream is discussed.

## Act 6 — ESG + infrastructure (02:19-03:00) · frames 0150/0155/0172/0195/0180
Camp + local hire → tailings dam → river water → cheapest grid power → haul offsite.
Job: de-risk (people, waste, water, power each get one shot, then move on).

## Act 7 — Product split (02:56-03:25)
Direct industrial sales (foundry, brakes, plastics, rubber, drilling, grease, lubes)
vs downstream value-add; downstream site needs renewables + road/rail + workforce.
Job: two revenue doors; cash now, upside later.

## Act 8 — Technology choice (03:20-03:55) · frame 0225
Flowsheets compared → thermal wins (purity + economics) → granulate/calcine →
one reactor → **99.999% @ ~2800°C, single step**. Job: rigour signal.

## Act 9 — Premium products (03:53-04:15) · frame 0235
Mill/classify 3 sizes → cathode enhancer (primary batteries), lead-acid anode,
nuclear moderator. Job: price premium justification.

## Act 10 — Battery roadmap (04:06-04:47) · frame 0265
Pilot spheronisation mill (qualification now) → reactors 2-3 in yr 3 → full
24 mills + coating → CSPG for EV anodes, matched to market penetration.
Job: staged capex; anode dream without overpromising day one.

## Act 11 — Logistics + close (04:44-05:11) · frame 0295
Container + rail across Asia/Europe, ship to N. America → "green energy revolution".
Job: globe bookend mirroring the open; mission statement as last line.
