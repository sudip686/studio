# Product Definition - presentationCreator

## Overview
presentationCreator is a sophisticated 3D geospatial presentation platform designed for the mining and geological sectors. It enables professionals to transform complex geological data—such as boreholes, block models, and terrain—into interactive, cinematic, and highly informative presentations for stakeholders.

## Target Audience
- Mining Engineers and Geologists
- Project Managers and Stakeholders
- Investors and Regulatory Bodies
- Exploration Teams

## Core Goals
- **Immersive Storytelling:** Provide a cinematic experience that guides viewers through geological narratives using animated transitions and "chapters."
- **Data-Driven Insight:** Integrate disparate data sources (GeoJSON, KML, block models) into a unified, interactive 3D environment.
- **Enhanced Collaboration:** Facilitate multi-stakeholder reviews and discussions through shared visualizations.
- **Professional Delivery:** Maintain a clean, professional aesthetic that balances cinematic quality with technical precision.

## Key Features
- **Interactive 3D Visualization:** High-fidelity rendering of geological models, boreholes, and topography using Cesium and Three.js.
- **Cinematic Chapters:** A structured presentation flow with animated transitions between key data views.
- **Geospatial Integration:** Support for standard formats (KML, GeoJSON) and specialized mining exports.
- **Technical Overlays:** Data-dense overlays (legends, scales, charts) that provide scientific context without cluttering the view.

## Visual Identity
- **Aesthetic:** A hybrid approach combining a clean, professional corporate look with high-fidelity, cinematic elements.
- **Theme:** Primarily dark-themed to enhance the contrast and visibility of 3D geological models.
- **Design Principles:** Material Design for UI components, ensuring accessibility and familiarity.
